Gene–Disease Association Prediction Using Protein Sequence

Co-authored undergraduate thesis exploring protein language models for computational biology. Combined ProtBERT protein sequence embeddings with BioBERT disease-text embeddings and trained an XGBoost classifier to predict gene–disease association strength on a 36,733-pair dataset built from DisGeNET and UniProt.
